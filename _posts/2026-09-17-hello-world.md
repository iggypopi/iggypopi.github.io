I write everything down. Between a memory I can't trust, ADHD, and OCD,
writing things down isn't a habit for me. It's a necessity. Over the
years those notes grew into a second brain, and the people who read them
kept telling me they got real value out of them. So I'm opening it up
to everyone.

Two rules run this place.

## Rule #1: Write everything down

If it isn't written down, it didn't happen. Every bug I chase, every
technique I learn, and every rabbit hole I fall into ends up in my
notes. The posts here are those notes, cleaned up and shared, so that
you don't have to start from scratch (and neither does future me).

## Rule #2: Explain it like I'm five

There's an idea often credited to physicist Richard Feynman: if you
can't explain something simply, you don't really understand it. I hold
every post to that standard. Each one starts from the ground up, basics
first, and climbs one step at a time.

You won't need to Google a term or ask an AI what something means
halfway through. Everything you need is right here, from zero to hero.

Welcome to my second brain. Let's dig in.
